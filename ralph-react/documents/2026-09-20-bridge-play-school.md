# Bridge Play School

Status: implemented locally on 2026-09-20.

## Purpose

Bridge Play School is the free, deterministic teaching layer for the Player. It
teaches pattern recognition and decision habits without paid AI calls. A learner
is shown the relevant hand and public information, commits to one of three
answers, and then sees an original explanation plus a graphical outline around
the important cards.

## Content and rights

The course does not copy teaching-site PBNs or prose. Public curriculum indexes
were used only to check topic coverage. Every included deal is generated by this
repository from original constraints and every explanation is original. This is
deliberately stricter than assuming that a publicly visible PBN is free to
republish.

## Curriculum

The first release has 45 lessons grouped into:

- planning, winner/loser counting and establishing suits;
- entries, unblocking, ducking and hold-up play;
- simple, repeated, two-way and double finesses;
- percentage suit play, restricted choice and safety plays;
- drawing or delaying trumps, short-hand ruffs, crossruffs, loser-on-loser,
  ruffing finesses and dummy reversal;
- avoidance, strip/endplay and simple squeeze recognition;
- opening leads, second- and third-hand play, attitude, count, suit preference
  and defensive unblocking;
- ACOL bidding using the app’s agreed 12–14 1NT foundation, Stayman, transfers,
  invitations, raises, weak twos, overcalls, take-out and negative doubles, and
  the strong 2C opening.

## Runtime design

- `/lessons` is a filterable catalogue with local progress.
- The whole course is passed to the existing Player through the same temporary
  session handoff used by competition packs.
- Card-play lessons open at the contract checkpoint. Bidding lessons open in the
  normal South practice auction.
- A scripted opening lead creates the intended teaching position; all later
  computer decisions use the existing local human-style DDS engine.
- The AI Coach is disabled for tutorial content. No login, API key, network call
  or usage allowance is involved.
- Completion state is stored under `ralph-tutorial-progress-v1` in localStorage.
- The catalogue can download the complete generated course as a PBN file.

## Future improvements
